1. Perspective-Invariant Road Geometry & Optical Scale

Standard CCTV cameras view road segments under oblique perspective distortion. Any static pixel threshold leads to false positives in the foreground and missed detections in the distance. We resolve this by anchoring all measurements to the local optical band height $H(x)$.

Equation 1.1 & 1.2: Local Road Band Height & Normalization
GEOMETRIC SCALE
$$H(x) = y_{\text{near}}(x) - y_{\text{far}}(x)$$ $$s(x) = \frac{H(x)}{H_{\text{ref}}}$$
Symbol Description Typical Bounds
$y_{\text{near}}(x)$ Lower polyline coordinate of the road pavement boundary Pavement bottom edge
$y_{\text{far}}(x)$ Upper polyline coordinate representing the road horizon/curb Pavement top edge
$H(x)$ Physical band height (in pixels) at horizontal position $x$ $60\,\text{px} \le H(x) \le 400\,\text{px}$
$s(x)$ Dimensionless perspective scaling factor relative to reference height $0.5 \le s(x) \le 2.0$

2. Temporal Median Asphalt Background Model

Rather than relying on resource-intensive Gaussian Mixture Models (GMM) that memorize stationary vehicles at traffic lights, `traffic-stats-kit` computes a robust temporal median over a uniform frame slice $\mathcal{S}$:

Equation 2.1 & 2.2: Median Background & Difference Operator
TEMPORAL ROBUSTNESS
$$\mathcal{B}(x, y) = \text{median}_{t \in \mathcal{S}} \Big\{ \mathcal{I}(x, y, t) \Big\}$$ $$\mathcal{D}(x, y, t) = \big| \mathcal{I}(x, y, t) - \mathcal{B}(x, y) \big|$$ $$\mathcal{M}(x, y, t) = \begin{cases} 1, & \mathcal{D}(x, y, t) \ge \tau_{\text{thresh}} \\ 0, & \mathcal{D}(x, y, t) < \tau_{\text{thresh}} \end{cases}$$
Parameter Definition Empirical Value
$\mathcal{B}(x, y)$ Clean reference asphalt model with vehicles removed Computed over $N \ge 60$ frames
$\mathcal{D}(x, y, t)$ Absolute foreground luminance delta Pixel intensity scale $[0, 255]$
$\tau_{\text{thresh}}$ Dynamic noise cut-off threshold $\tau \in [25, 45]$ intensity levels

3. Dual-Line Verification Protocol

A single detection line suffers from false crossings triggered by oscillating power lines, branch shadows, and headlight beam extensions. The two-line protocol requires an optical mass to break two distinct spatial lines in chronological order:

Equation 3.1: Chronological Verification Gate
TRAJECTORY INTEGRATION
$$\text{Pass}_{\text{valid}} \iff \exists \, t_1, t_2 \quad \text{such that} \quad x(t_1) = x_{\text{count}}, \; x(t_2) = x_{\text{verify}}, \; t_2 > t_1$$ $$\text{Direction} = \text{sgn}\big(x(t_2) - x(t_1)\big) = \begin{cases} +1 \;\; (\text{LR}), & x(t_2) > x(t_1) \\ -1 \;\; (\text{RL}), & x(t_2) < x(t_1) \end{cases}$$

4. In-Frame Articulated Vehicle Merging

Articulated trucks (tractor + semi-trailer) exhibit a visual gap above the fifth wheel where road background shows through. This splits a single truck into two false consecutive vehicles. We enforce **in-frame spatial clustering** before generating timestamps:

Equation 4.1 & 4.2: Articulated Coupling Conditions
SCALE-NORMALIZED CLUSTERING
$$\Delta x_{\text{gap}} = |x_1 - x_2| - \frac{w_1 + w_2}{2} \le \gamma_{\text{gap}} \cdot H(x), \quad \gamma_{\text{gap}} = 0.45$$ $$\Delta y_{\text{contact}} = |y_{1,\text{ground}} - y_{2,\text{ground}}| \le \gamma_{\text{y}} \cdot H(x), \quad \gamma_{\text{y}} = 0.15$$
Criterion Analytical Formulation Civil Engineering Justification
Gap Distance $\Delta x_{\text{gap}} \le 0.45 \cdot H(x)$ Inter-vehicle coupling clearance is under $2.2\,\text{m}$
Ground Contact $\Delta y_{\text{contact}} \le 0.15 \cdot H(x)$ Wheels of cab and trailer share the identical road grade

5. Headway Distribution & Traffic Flow Theory (HCM Standards)

Following the Highway Capacity Manual (HCM) standards, headway between vehicle $i$ and $i-1$ is tracked continuously:

Equation 5.1, 5.2 & 5.3: Headway, PHF & Occupancy
HCM STANDARD
$$h_i = t_i - t_{i-1} \quad [\text{seconds}]$$ $$\text{PHF} = \frac{V_{\text{hour}}}{4 \times V_{15,\max}}$$ $$\rho_{\text{occ}} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^N \Delta t_{\text{occupancy}, i}}{T_{\text{period}}} \times 100\%$$
Indicator Engineering Criterion Safety & Flow Insight
Headway Floor $h_i \ge 1.0\,\text{s}$ for free-flow $h_i < 1.0\,\text{s}$ with $>5\%$ frequency indicates split trailers
Peak Hour Factor $0.75 \le \text{PHF} \le 0.95$ Measures uniformity of traffic flow within peak volume hours
Time Occupancy $\rho_{\text{occ}} < 18\%$ (Level of Service A/B) Ratio of time virtual inductance loop is covered by metal

6. Astronomical Solar Noon Clock Drift Correction

Roadside DVRs frequently lose RTC synchronization due to power cuts. We realign all timestamps to true astronomical local time using dawn and dusk light thresholds:

Equation 6.1: Ephemeris Timestamp Recovery
ASTRONOMICAL RTC ALIGNMENT
$$t_{\text{midpoint}} = \frac{t_{\text{dawn}} + t_{\text{dusk}}}{2}$$ $$\Delta t_{\text{RTC}} = t_{\text{midpoint}} - t_{\text{solar\_noon}}(\phi, \lambda, \text{date})$$
Variable Definition
$t_{\text{dawn}}, t_{\text{dusk}}$ Average frame intensity crossing threshold $\tau_{\text{solar}}$ at sunrise/sunset
$t_{\text{solar\_noon}}(\phi, \lambda, \text{date})$ Theoretical astronomical solar transit calculated from latitude and longitude
$\Delta t_{\text{RTC}}$ Offset applied uniformly to all video clip timestamps

7. The 4 Non-Negotiable Validation Gates

Gate 1
Contact Sheet Audit
Every single counted vehicle is rendered onto contact_sheet.png. 100% of tiles must depict a verified vehicle.
Gate 2
Headway Floor (< 1.0s)
Consecutive passes with $h_i < 1.0\,\text{s}$ must be $\le 5\%$. Any anomaly flags unmerged multi-axle combinations.
Gate 3
Coverage Gate (> 95%)
Continuous footage continuity must exceed 95% of the survey window. Power drops are explicitly logged.
Gate 4
Bit-Exact Determinism
Repeated pipeline execution over identical input video yields bit-exact CSV timeline entries across all runs.
